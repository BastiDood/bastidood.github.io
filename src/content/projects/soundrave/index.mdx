[SoundRave](https://github.com/BastiDood/soundrave) was an aggregator app for new music released by a user's followed artists on Spotify. At the time, the news feed was missing from Spotify's features. The app aimed to fill in this hole.

The <dfn><abbr title="User Interface">UI</abbr></dfn> is primarily rendered by the server via the [Express](https://expressjs.com/) framework for [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/). For data persistence, [MongoDB](https://www.mongodb.com/) is used as the database and cache for Spotify <dfn><abbr title="Application Program Interface">API</abbr></dfn> calls.
