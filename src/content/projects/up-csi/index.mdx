The [UP Center for Student Innovations](https://up-csi.org/) is a student-oriented organization aimed towards the enhancement of student learning from the [University of the Philippines](https://up.edu.ph) [Diliman](https://upd.edu.ph/), [Department of Computer Science](https://dcs.upd.edu.ph/) by working with real-world projects. As part of my 2023-2024 term as the Director for Engineering, I was tasked to lead the development and deployment of the organization's official website—a project that had previously been postponed over the years.

The website is an [open-source](https://github.com/up-csi/up-csi.github.io) static website powered by [SvelteKit](https://kit.svelte.dev/) and developed in the open by UP CSI's members.
