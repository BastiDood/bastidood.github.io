[<dfn><abbr title="Helpdesk and Ticketing Dashboard">HATiD</abbr></dfn>](https://github.com/BastiDood/hatid) is an open-source Helpdesk and Ticketing Dashboard powered by [SvelteKit](https://kit.svelte.dev/) that aims to modernize ticketing systems such as the popular [osTicket](https://osticket.com/). The project was initially developed as part of the 2023 internship program at the [University of the Philippines](https://up.edu.ph/) [Diliman](https://upd.edu.ph/), [Department of Industrial Engineering and Operations Research](https://ieor.engg.upd.edu.ph/).
